Privacy Policy for Buddy Habit Tracker

This app stores all of its data on your device. This includes your habits, your Buddies, your progress, and your badges.

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with the developer or with third parties. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking, and it does not require an account or sign-in.

If you delete the app, its stored data is removed from your device.
